Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

This policy covers the Ascend Path app. It explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • Training and progress data — module completion, practice repetitions, readiness reviews, and journal entries you submit within the app.
  • Teacher/student relationships — if you connect with a teacher or student account, limited progress and journal data may be shared between those linked accounts.

How we use this information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account session.
  • To track and display your training progress and readiness status.
  • To enable teacher oversight features you or your linked account opt into.
  • To respond to support requests.

Third-party services

We use Google Sign-In for authentication and Supabase for data storage and authentication infrastructure. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Data retention

We retain account and training data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. Revoking the app's access from your Google account settings disables sign-in but does not automatically delete data already stored — contact us to complete deletion.

Contact
